### ISTQB CTFL syllabus v4.0.1

Copyright © ISTQB and the syllabus authors, "All rights reserved". The copyright notice (page 2) grants these conditions of use:

* Extracts, for **non-commercial** use, may be copied if the source is acknowledged.
* Any individual or group may use the syllabus **as the basis for articles and books**, if the authors and ISTQB are acknowledged as the source and copyright owners.
* Accredited training providers may base a course on it; advertising it as syllabus-based needs official accreditation.

**How QALAB uses it:** the lessons are the team's own explanations and examples, built on the syllabus structure and terms (the "basis for articles" condition). No passage is copied verbatim; only short labels such as principle names and section titles are reused. Every lesson ends with a Sources section naming the syllabus version and sections, with "© International Software Testing Qualifications Board (ISTQB®) and the syllabus authors".

**Open points for a human to decide:**

1. QALAB is a personal learning platform. If it ever becomes commercial, or is presented as ISTQB exam preparation, the "non-commercial" and "accredited training provider" conditions must be reviewed again, possibly with ISTQB.
2. QALAB must not say it is ISTQB-accredited or official. The Sources line says "based on", which is accurate.
3. ISTQB® is a registered trademark: use the name only to identify the source.
